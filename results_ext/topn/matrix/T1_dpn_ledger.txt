DP/DPN > 1 means DPN is faster.
gain retained = (BL/DPN - 1)/(BL/DP - 1): the share of full DP's benefit that DPN keeps.
  >100% means DPN beat full DP (its rotation is 4.3-4.7x fresher for the sinks it tracks).
  It is a ratio of small numbers, so read DP/DPN for how close the policies are:
  a 1.8% delay difference on VGG becomes a 25-point difference here.
  NOTE: distinct from 'capture' in the paper, which is DP's share of the BL->oracle reduction.

N@95 changes nothing on any workload (paired p = 0.66 / 0.50 / 0.46): gain is flat in N
from 23 to 108.  One cell is the exception — DeiT's deepest placement (5.9x free-flow)
starves at N@90, DP/DPN 0.761, and recovers to 0.944 at N@95.
