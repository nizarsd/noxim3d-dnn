\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:related}
Communication is a recognised bottleneck of DNN accelerators. Dataflow
accelerators attack it by scheduling data reuse so that fewer bytes
move~\cite{chenEyerissSpatialArchitecture2016,szeEfficientProcessingDeep2017};
IMC accelerators attack it by keeping weights stationary in crossbar
tiles~\cite{shafieeISAACConvolutionalNeural2016,chiPRIMENovelProcessinginMemory2016,longFerroelectricFETBasedProcessinginMemory2019},
which removes weight traffic but relocates the rest onto the on-chip network,
making the interconnect the design object~\cite{nabavinejadOverviewEfficientInterconnection2020}.

The closest precedent is Krishnan et
al.~\cite{krishnanImpactOnchipInterconnect2022}, who map DNN layers to tiles
on a mesh and drive the NoC with per-pair injection rates, the traffic model
adopted here, and conclude that the network is uncongested at average rates
--- a conclusion about the mean that Sec.~\ref{sec:r2} qualifies. The same
group models IMC and its NoC end to
end~\cite{krishnanSIAMChipletbasedScalable2021} and synthesises a NoC per
DNN~\cite{mandalLatencyOptimizedReconfigurableNoC2020}, taking topology as
the variable where this work keeps a regular 3D mesh and varies routing and
selection. On 3D NoCs, TTNNM~\cite{liTTNNMThermalTrafficAware2024} and
TEFLON~\cite{narangTEFLONThermallyEfficient2024} optimise placement or fabric
with the packing given; adaptive routing and
selection~\cite{dahirHighlyAdaptiveDeadlockFree2013,makDPnetworkOptimalDynamic2009,choiOnChipCommunicationNetwork2018}
have been developed largely on synthetic traffic. At wafer scale the
Cerebras WSE provisions more fabric than memory
bandwidth~\cite{miyajimaBenchmarkingCerebrasWafer2025}: past a certain
integration the problem is communication-centric by construction.

Each of these works optimises one layer with the others held fixed: packing
for utilisation or traffic volume, placement for hops or heat, routing for a
given placement. None asks how a choice at one layer changes what the next can
achieve. The results below show that this is where the largest effects lie:
the minimum-traffic packing is the wrong one, placement matters only in a
regime that packing decides, and what selection yields depends on both.
